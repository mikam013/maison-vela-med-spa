What is Dermal Filler?

Hyaluronic acid filler replaces the volume that fades with age. In the right hands it lifts, contours and smooths without looking ‘done’.


We use a cannula where we can, which means fewer pokes and less bruising.


Who it’s for

  • Flat or hollow cheeks
  • Smile lines and marionette lines
  • A softer jawline or chin
  • Tired-looking under-eyes

What to expect

  1. Consultation and photos
  2. Numbing cream for 15 minutes
  3. Filler placed slowly, checking the mirror with you as we go
  4. Aftercare plan and a free 2-week review

Questions

How long does filler last?

Between 9 and 18 months depending on the area and the product.

Can it be reversed?

Yes. Hyaluronic acid filler can be dissolved if you ever want to undo it.

Will I bruise?

Some people do. Skip alcohol, fish oil and ibuprofen for 3 days before to lower the chance.
