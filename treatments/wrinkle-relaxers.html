What is Wrinkle Relaxers?

Wrinkle relaxers calm the small muscles that crease your skin when you frown, squint or raise your brows. Lines soften, makeup sits better, and you look rested rather than frozen.


We map your face before we inject and start conservatively. Most first-timers use 20–40 units. You can always add more at your free two-week check.


Who it’s for

  • Forehead lines and the ‘11s’ between your brows
  • Crow’s feet that stay when you stop smiling
  • Preventing lines in your late 20s and 30s
  • Lip flips and gummy smiles

What to expect

  1. Face mapping and a quick chat about your goals
  2. Cleanse and a few tiny injections (most people say it feels like a pinch)
  3. Back to your day. Avoid lying flat or working out for 4 hours
  4. Free check at 2 weeks to fine-tune

Questions

Does it hurt?

Most clients rate it a 2 out of 10. We use very fine needles and ice if you want it.

When will I see results?

You’ll notice a change in 3–5 days and the full result at 14 days.

Will I look frozen?

Not with us. We dose for movement, not a mask. Your friends will say you look rested.
